## How labels were verified

Helper scripts are in `content/tools/` (`commons.py`, `scan.py`, `scan_real.py`, `verify.py`, `fetch.py`, `build_items.py`). All requests used the Commons API with a descriptive User-Agent, one at a time with pauses.

**Real photos (24).** Each file had to meet all of these, checked again by `fetch.py` just before download:
- uploaded to Commons before 2021-01-01 (the earliest is 2007, the latest December 2020);
- camera Make and Model present in the original file's EXIF (recorded in each item's clue and provenance note);
- in a Commons *Quality images* or *Featured pictures* category (all 24 are);
- a named photographer who is also the uploader or is credited on the file page.

**AI images (24).** Each file had to meet both of these, checked by reading the file page wikitext:
- the description, author field or licence template names the AI model (e.g. `{{PD-algorithm}}` plus 'Adobe Firefly Image 3', 'Midjourney version 6', 'DALL-E 3', 'Stable Diffusion 3.5 Large', 'StyleGAN2');
- the file is in an AI-generated-images category (e.g. *Images generated by Midjourney*, *Animals by DALL-E*, *Images generated by Stable Diffusion*, *AI-generated landscapes*, *Images generated by thispersondoesnotexist.com*).
Images described as AI *edits* of real photos (e.g. ChatGPT-retouched buildings, Gemini versions of real buses) were left out, because they are partly real.

**Content exclusions.** No famous people, politicians or identifiable real people in AI images. No minors as the main subject, nudity, violence, logos as the main subject or news imagery. The four real portraits show unnamed adults: a Hamburg police officer, a market worker, a street snake charmer and a bride. The four AI portraits are fictional people.

**Clues.** Each clue was written after viewing the saved image, and checked again after the 4:3 crop. Clues whose detail was cropped out were removed or re-pointed. Where an AI image shows no clear artefact (e.g. `green-apple-tablecloth`), the clues say so and rely on source and context. `reviewStatus` is `ai_draft`: a human should review the clues before switching it to `reviewed`.

- **CC BY / CC BY-SA items** must be credited with author, licence and a link wherever they are shown (the game's source panel does this through `attribution`, `license` and `sourceUrl`). The share-alike items (CC BY-SA 2.5 / 3.0 / 3.0 PL / 4.0) are cropped and re-encoded, which counts as an adaptation, so the modified image files must stay under the same licence.
- `black-bear-yellow-flowers` is an AI image but its uploader licensed it CC BY 2.0 on Flickr. Credit Kandukuru Nagarjun.
- Several AI files carry both PD-algorithm and a CC licence added by the uploader (e.g. CC BY 4.0 on `woman-saree-welcome-pose` and `mutton-curry-clay-bowl`, CC BY-SA 4.0 on `white-computer-curved-screen`). Both are recorded. To be safe, credit the uploader as if the CC licence applies.
- Public-domain and CC0 items need no attribution, but it is given anyway.
